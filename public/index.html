How each metric is measured (read this once)

Reachability / RTT — timed HTTP request to the device's IP and port. An answer of any kind (page, redirect, auth challenge, protocol mismatch) proves the device is alive on the LAN and gives round-trip time.

Jitter — average variation between consecutive RTTs. Above ~30 ms video stutters even when speed looks fine.

Loss — probes that never answered within the timeout. Anything above 1% on a wired camera link means a cable, port or interference problem.

Throughput — repeatedly pulls the snapshot image (cameras) and measures bytes/second. For routers, switches and APs with no image endpoint, the tool falls back to page-fetch timing and reports latency-grade only.

RTSP / stream ports — browsers cannot speak RTSP, so port 554 (and 8554, 37777, 34567, 8000) is tested for reachability only: a fast rejection means the port is open and talking a non-HTTP protocol; a timeout means blocked or closed.

Offline — no internet, CDN, tracking or server. Everything runs inside this page on your LAN.
